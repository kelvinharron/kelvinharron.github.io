Forever support

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Forever keeps the people you care about, living or passed, and the dates that come round for them every year. If something isn’t working, the answer is probably below.

Still stuck? Email kelvinharron+foreverapp@gmail.com and I’ll write back.

My people aren’t on my other device

Forever keeps your people in step through your own iCloud. On each device:

  • Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account.
  • Make sure iCloud is turned on for Forever in the Settings app, under your name → iCloud.
  • Open Forever’s Settings and look at Your keepsakes. It says whether your people are being kept on all your devices.

Changes usually arrive within a minute or two, sometimes longer on a slow connection. Without iCloud, Forever still works fully on the one device.

My reminders aren’t arriving

  • Check notifications are allowed for Forever in the Settings app. If they’re off, Forever’s Settings will say so and take you there.
  • In Forever’s Settings, under Reminders, check the time of day and that Birthdays, Anniversaries and Remembrance are on.
  • Someone who’s muted gets no reminders. Long press them on Home or in People to unmute.

Birthdays get a note a week ahead and on the day. Anniversaries and remembrance days get one on the day.

How many people can I keep for free?

Ten people to celebrate. The people you remember are always free and never count. Room for everyone makes room for as many as you like: it’s paid once, there’s no subscription, and it’s shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I’ve already bought Room for everyone

Open Forever’s Settings, tap Room for everyone, then Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. If someone in your family bought it, make sure Purchase Sharing is on in Family Sharing.

Apple handles payments, so refunds are through Apple’s Report a Problem.

Where are gift idea suggestions?

They’re written on your device by Apple Intelligence, so they only appear on iPhones and iPads that support it. Everywhere else, the gift ideas are the ones you write.

Getting everything out, or removing someone

  • Everything as a file: Settings → Export everything gives you your people, memories and photos.
  • Removing someone: open their page and choose Remove from Forever.

The privacy policy explains what Forever keeps and where.
