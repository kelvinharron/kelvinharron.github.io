Forever privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Forever is made by Kelvin Harron. This policy explains what happens to the things you keep in Forever.

The short version: they’re yours, they stay with you, and I never see them.

What Forever keeps

The people you add, their dates, the memories, quotes, likes and gift ideas you write down, and the photos you choose. Forever also remembers your settings, such as your reminder time and app icon.

Where it’s kept

  • On your device. Everything lives in a database on your iPhone or iPad. Forever’s widgets read the same database so they can show your people on the Home Screen.
  • In your own iCloud, if you’re signed in. Forever uses Apple’s CloudKit to keep your people in step across your devices. It’s stored in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. I can’t see it, and neither can anyone else. Apple’s privacy policy covers iCloud.

There is no Forever server, and no Forever account.

Contacts

If you choose to search your contacts, Forever asks for permission and then looks through them on your device to help you pick someone. Only the people you pick are kept, and only their name, photo and dates. You can also add people by hand, or pick someone with Apple’s contact picker without giving Forever access at all.

Photos

Forever uses Apple’s photo picker, so it only ever receives the photos you choose. It never sees the rest of your library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device from the dates you’ve kept. Nothing is sent from a server.

Gift ideas

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Forever can suggest gift ideas using Apple’s on-device model. What you’ve written about someone never leaves your device to make them. On other devices there are no suggestions, and the gift ideas are the ones you write.

Purchases

Room for everyone is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Forever only learns whether it’s been bought, so it can make room. I don’t receive your name, your payment details or anything else about you.

What Forever doesn’t do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services that collect data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

If you choose to share a memory or export your data, it goes wherever you send it with the share sheet, and that’s up to you.

Your choices

  • See everything: Settings → Export everything gives you all of it as a file.
  • Delete someone: open their page and choose Remove from Forever.
  • Delete everything: delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (wording varies by iOS version), choose Forever and delete its data.
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud for Forever in the Settings app.

Children

Forever isn’t aimed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Forever ever starts collecting anything, it will say so clearly in the app first.
